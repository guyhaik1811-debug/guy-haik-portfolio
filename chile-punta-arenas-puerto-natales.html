Punta Arenas and Puerto Natales, October 2017

October 22 to 24, 2017Guy Haik

I reached Punta Arenas, in the far south of Chile, on the evening of Sunday, October 22, 2017, after a day on the road from Ushuaia. This chapter covers the ferry across the Strait of Magellan, my first walk along the Punta Arenas waterfront, and a short stop in Puerto Natales two days later. Puerto Natales is the town most travellers use as their base for Torres del Paine National Park, the famous trekking area in Chilean Patagonia, so this stop was the start of my Torres del Paine trek, which has its own chapter. My photos from October 22 run from 15:31 to 20:17 by my phone's clock, and Google Photos places the later ones in Punta Arenas, Chile. Boxes like the one below hold research I did in November 2026, with a source and a date, so you can tell it apart from what I remember.

Ushuaia to Punta Arenas by road (checked November 2026)

Duration: Swoop Patagonia describes the bus as 11 to 12 hours, including a ferry across the narrowest point of the Strait of Magellan, and calls it the simplest and cheapest option. The page gives no prices, schedule or crossing details (Swoop Patagonia, undated, copyright 2026).

The ferry and the border: a trip report from March 2018, a few months after mine, says the crossing has no pier and the ramp leads straight into the sea, that passengers stay inside their vehicle, and that nothing crossed one day because of a stormy sea. It also mentions about an hour and a half of waiting at the Argentine border and about half an hour at the Chilean one, with backpacks X-rayed (GigaPlaces, March 13, 2018). That is one traveller's account from 2018, so check current waiting times before you plan a tight connection.

I did not find a current price or timetable for the ferry, and I did not check which company operates it today.

What to know today, in short (checked November 2026)
  • Ushuaia to Punta Arenas by road: 11 to 12 hours including a ferry across the Strait of Magellan. I found no current price, timetable or border waiting times, so do not plan a tight connection.
  • Punta Arenas to Puerto Natales: about 3 hours 15 minutes by bus. One company lists 11 daily departures from September to April and 7 from May to August, with no prices. Book early in high season.
  • The ship monument: I think it is the Monument to the Crew of the Schooner Ancud, but I matched it from the photo, so it is likely, not certain.

The ship monument on the waterfront (checked November 2026)

The next photo shows a sculpture of the bow of a ship with a flag, figures, a cannon, and a goat and a rooster on top. It looks like the Monument to the Crew of the Schooner Ancud on the Costanera del Estrecho. It is a 6-metre work by Guillermo Merino Pedrero, inaugurated in 2014, about three years before my visit. It commemorates the 1843 expedition from Ancud that established Chilean sovereignty over the strait, and it includes 23 expedition members and animals from the Chilote tradition (GPSmyCity, undated). I am matching it from the photo and the description, so treat the identification as likely, not certain.

October 24: Puerto Natales

I have no photos from Monday, October 23, and I do not remember how I spent that day. On Tuesday, October 24, I was in Puerto Natales, a town on the water about three hours' drive north of Punta Arenas and the usual gateway to Torres del Paine National Park, and Google Photos places my two photos from that evening there, at 19:16 and 19:17. In Puerto Natales I stayed in a hostel run by a local man who speaks Hebrew. I found him through the Israeli travel site Lametayel. I am not naming him or the hostel here, and I do not know if it is still running. I do not remember what I did during the day, or how I travelled from Punta Arenas.

Punta Arenas to Puerto Natales by bus (checked November 2026)

The Bus Sur page lists about 3 hours and 15 minutes. From September to April it shows 11 daily departures, from 7:00 to 21:00, and from May to August 7 departures. It says schedules may vary with the season and operation, and it recommends booking early in high season. The page gives no prices and no update date (torresdelpaine.com, Bus Sur). I did not check other companies.
