Southeast Asia · Thailand · Ko Phi Phi

Ko Phi Phi, September 2019

From September 8, 2019Guy Haik

I spent seven nights on Ko Phi Phi in September 2019, from the ferry on September 8 to the ferry back on September 15. This page is built from my photos, my phone's clock and what I remember, and I say plainly where I do not remember.

My conclusion up front: Phi Phi was a mix of nights out and quiet days, and the quiet days are the ones I would repeat. The photos pin only to the island as a whole, so I do not name places I cannot read on a sign or that I do not remember. The boxes hold what I looked up in November 2026, marked as current.

What to know today, in short (checked November 2026)
  • Viewpoint: about 30 to 50 baht for viewpoints 1 and 2, depending on the source, and 20 baht more for viewpoint 3. Bring small cash.
  • Boat tours: a full-day shared long-tail "7 islands" tour is listed at about 1,300 baht, and the national park fee of 400 baht per foreign adult is often not included and is paid in cash.
  • Ferry to Phuket: roughly 1 to 2 hours, from about 500 baht on the comparison sites I read. The first ferries leave around 09:00 and the last around 17:00.
  • My own 2019 prices: I do not remember any, so I give none.

The sources are in the box at the end. These things change, so check before you go.

September 8: arriving

I took the ferry from Krabi, and my first photos from the water are from 14:41. I do not remember the operator or what the ticket cost. The sea was gray, with storm clouds in places, and at about 17:07 I was at the pier.

That evening I went out. I have a photo of a bar at 22:35, a free-shots moment at 23:07 and a party from about 23:30. I do not remember the names of the places. At 00:33 on September 9 I took a selfie on the floor with a white kitten, and at 01:44 I photographed the beach at night with yellow boats on the sand.

September 9: a fire show

On the evening of September 9, between about 21:28 and 21:53, I filmed a fire show on the beach. It was crowded, so I do not show the clips. In one photo, a performer stands in front of a neon sign. I do not show it either, because the audience is in the frame.

September 10: boats and a fight night

On September 10 I went on a boat trip with an agency that called it "7 islands." I do not remember its name or the price. My photo from 15:19 is the one clear view I have, with a limestone wall and green water.

That night, at about 22:40, I filmed a friend's Muay Thai fight. I do not show it. I know that the fights on the island are for volunteers from the crowd, who are asked to come up, and I wrote what I remember in the section below. I did not confirm which bar it was.

September 11: the viewpoint

On September 11, from about 16:25 to 18:33, I was at the viewpoint above the village, roughly two hours in all. I climbed up through a private garden where you pay a small fee. At the first viewpoint there is a big "VIEWPOINT 1" sign and, on a wall, a banner with photos called "Before Tsunami" and "After Tsunami," showing the same bays.

At the top I took a selfie at 16:39, and I saw macaque monkeys on the rocks at about 17:36. I stayed until dusk, and the light went from white to gray to a thin pink over the bay. I do not have a photo of a real sunset, only the last light.

At 19:57 I took two photos of my hostel room: a double bed, a wicker nightstand, an air conditioner, a TV and a small fridge. It is the room I stayed in. I do not remember the hostel's name or the price. That night I filmed a beach party until after 23:57, and I do not show those clips.

September 12: Long Beach

On September 12 I got to know a friend, someone I met after getting off the ferry. We walked together toward Long Beach, and on the way we stopped at a school, spontaneously. There were children in the yard and in a classroom, so I do not show most of the photos. Here are the sports yard, the welcome sign and a tsunami evacuation sign, cropped so that nobody is in the frame.

From about 12:31 to 15:54 I walked the road and the beach. I passed bungalows among trees, a dirt road under construction, and an empty beach at low tide. I do not remember whether the bungalows were where I stayed on other nights. I do not know what the road was being built for.

At night I went to a bar with live music. Its sign looks like a name with "Siam" in it, but I did not confirm it, so I do not give the name. I filmed the band at 23:55 and a dancing crowd just after midnight, and I do not show those clips.

September 13: a quiet day

September 13 was quiet and had no party. At low tide, around 16:11 and in the hours after, the bay was nearly empty. There were kayaks and long-tail boats on the sand, and a large blue boat was resting on the beach. At dusk I took photos of two small spirit shrines and an empty alley, and at about 18:19 I lay in a hammock on the sand.

September 14 and 15: leaving

On September 14, at 21:52, I photographed a handwritten note of Thai airlines: THAI, Thai Lion Air, Air Asia, THAI Smile, Nok Air and Bangkok Airways. I do not know who wrote it. I do not know why it was written. I also have restaurant photos from that day with people, which I do not show.

On September 15 I took a ferry from Phi Phi to Phuket. I do not remember the price. I have no photos from that day. I slept the last night in Thailand in a hostel very close to the airport, and I do not remember its name. On September 16 I left for the next destination. I describe it in the next chapter.

Phi Phi: what I looked up (checked November 2026)
  • Viewpoint: one guide says 30 baht covers viewpoints 1 and 2 and viewpoint 3 costs 20 more (Thaizer). Another lists 50 baht for 1 and 2 (Thai Holiday Guide). It also gives the climb as 30 to 45 minutes. The prices differ, so bring a little extra cash.
  • Long-tail "7 islands" tour: a day tour from Phi Phi is listed at 1,300 baht per adult, 9 hours, and the 400 baht national park fee is excluded (Expedia Thailand).
  • The fight bar: travel writers describe a bar in Tonsai Village with a ring where tourists fight for a free bucket of drinks (Nomadic Matt). I did not confirm that it is the bar I went to, or what it charges at the door.
  • Ferry to Phuket: comparison sites such as Rome2rio, Direct Ferries and Ferryscanner show crossings of about 1 to 3 hours, from roughly 500 baht. First departures are around 08:00 to 09:00 and the last around 17:00. The sea can be choppy, and larger ferries are steadier than speedboats.
  • What I did not find: any price from 2019, or a current price for my hostel, because I do not remember its name.

These are prices as listed today, not what I paid.

What I think now

On the fighting: in the Muay Thai bar, as I recall, they ask volunteers from the crowd. It is free, the equipment is provided, and there is a short warm-up. I come from the world of MMA, and for me it was fun. I think it depends on whom you ask. I think there is only one fight club on the island, but I did not confirm that.

What I do not remember

I do not remember the ferry operator or the price from Krabi, the name of my hostel, the name of the school, what we did there, which bars I went to, what I paid anywhere, what music was played, how the people at the school received us, or the name of the hostel near the Phuket airport.
